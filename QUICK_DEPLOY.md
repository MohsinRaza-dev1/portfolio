# 🚀 Quick Deploy - Get Permanent URL in 5 Minutes

## 🎯 **Step 1: Go to Vercel.com**
**URL:** https://vercel.com

## 🎯 **Step 2: Sign Up/Login**
- Click "Sign Up" or "Login"
- Use GitHub, Google, or email
- **FREE** - No credit card needed

## 🎯 **Step 3: Deploy Your Portfolio**
1. **Click "New Project"**
2. **"Import Git Repository"**
3. **Choose "Local Folder"**
4. **Select your mohsine folder**
5. **Click "Deploy"**

## 🎯 **Step 4: Get Your Permanent URL**
**Your URL will be:** `https://mohsin-portfolio.vercel.app`

---

## 📱 **Alternative: Drag & Drop Deploy**

1. **Build your portfolio:**
   - Open terminal in your project folder
   - Run: `npm run build`
   - Wait for build to complete

2. **Go to Netlify.com**
   - Sign up for FREE
   - Drag and drop the `out` folder
   - Get URL: `https://mohsin-portfolio.netlify.app`
